## What check_raise_freq Measures
Per-opponent frequency of postflop check-raises. A check-raise opportunity is when:
1. Opp's first action in a postflop round is 'check'.
2. We (my_id) raise/allin in the same round after the check.
3. Opp then has the option to raise/allin (= check-raise fired).

High check_raise_freq (>=0.30 with >=3 samples) indicates a value-heavy check-raiser
who traps with strong hands. Marginal hands should fold to their check-raises.
