# Float-Lead Offensive Mechanism — Rationale & Evidence

## Confirmed Universal Leak
Postflop avg_raise stuck at ~0.4x pot across 275+ matches. Turn raise 27-33% at 0.4x, river raise 9-17% at 0.4-0.6x. Target: 0.6-0.8x. The bot systematically under-bets in offensive spots.

## Root Cause: Missing Decision Point
When the opponent c-bets the flop, the bot calls (floats), and the opponent checks the turn — NO existing function handles this with proper sizing:

1. `should_probe_bet()` requires `_pfr_checked_previous_street()` — checks if PFR CHECKED the previous street. In this scenario, the PFR BET the flop (c-bet), so this returns False. Probe doesn't fire.
2. `passive_exploit_trigger()` "second_barrel" requires WE bet the flop (`_we_raised(history, 1)`). In this scenario, we CALLED. Doesn't fire.
3. Main `choose_raise()` path fires for strong hands but sizing gets capped: `induce_mode` caps nuts at ~0.40x, `probe_mode` caps bluffs at ~0.25x.

Result: the bot either checks back or under-bets in the most common "float then lead" spot.

## H2H Evidence
Worst matchups for v114 (all 10g samples, noisy but directional):
- vs v96: 0.20 wr — v96 is hyper-aggressive postflop (AF=13.9 flop), c-bets frequently, folds only 4% to flop raises. CALLING STATION that also bets → we need BIGGER value sizing.
- vs v93: 0.40 wr — similar profile (AF=14.6 flop).
- vs v111: 0.40 wr — similar (AF=10.8 flop).

All three opponents c-bet aggressively then sometimes give up on later streets. When they check the turn after c-betting the flop, their range is CAPPED. The bot should PUNISH this by leading with a polarized range at 0.6-0.75x pot.
