# Neural v141 Operator Data Archive

This directory contains historical v140/v141 diagnostic traces and native TCP
evaluation outputs produced on 2026-07-10. It is archived for reproducibility
only. The associated candidates were never completion-tagged and do not satisfy
the current signed 5+3x70 official EXE or live-pool strength requirements.

The archive is intentionally kept out of `main` and is recoverable through the
annotated tag `archive-neural-lab-v141-operator-data-20260711`.
